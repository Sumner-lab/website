**Soapbox Science** (co-founder with [Dr Nathalie Pettorelli](https://www.zsl.org/science/users/nathalie-pettorelli) (ZSL); 2011–present) is a multi-award-winning science engagement and equality initiative. Public outreach events bring female and gender-minority scientists to the streets to discuss their science, raising their public profiles and putting the issue of equality in science in front of a public audience. [soapboxscience.org](http://www.soapboxscience.org) — watch a [film about it](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eqC2DIB5Ccw).

**Athena SWAN.** Seirian was Academic Lead for the Bronze Athena SWAN award for the School of Biological Sciences, University of Bristol, and leads the Athena SWAN committee for the Division of Biosciences, UCL.
