We combine genomic, transcriptomic, and behavioural approaches to understand how complex insect societies evolve. Our work is organised around four themes:

- [Evolution](/research/evolution/) — the evolutionary processes that create and govern the diversity of insect life
- [Genomics and Bioinformatics](/research/genomics/) — building reproducible pipelines to explore the molecular basis of behaviour
- Mechanisms and Development *(coming soon)*
- [Ecology and Human Interactions](/research/ecology/) — how wasps shape ecosystems, and how people shape wasps' reputations
