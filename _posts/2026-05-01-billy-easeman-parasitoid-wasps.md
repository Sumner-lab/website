*January 2026–May 2026. UCL MRes student Billy Easeman on his MRes research project with the Sumner Lab.*

Wasps are an undervalued group of insects with high ecological importance as arthropod regulators, using two major strategies — as parasitoids (solitary, with a larval parasitic stage) and aculeates (predators) — both of which lack good diet information. Billy's project used DNA metabarcoding of wasp guts to better understand their diets, focusing on parasitoids (can we identify their larval-stage host from remnants of DNA in the guts of adults?) and African social wasps (what do the *Belonogaster* wasps of Cameroon hunt?). As part of his project, Billy visited our collaborator [Dr Jordan Cuff](https://www.ncl.ac.uk/nes/people/profile/jordancuff.html) at Newcastle University, a world expert in using nanopore sequencing to analyse arthropod gut contents.
