*Postdoc [Émeline Favreau]({{ site.baseurl }}/the-group/emeline-favreau/)* *on her experience attending the Nextflow Summit and Hackathon in October 2022*.

In October 2022, I was lucky to be encouraged by my research team to attend the [Nextflow Summit and Hackathon in Barcelona](https://nf-co.re/events/2022/hackathon-october-2022), with [Seqera Labs](https://www.linkedin.com/company/seqera-labs/). I wanted to improve my skills at the Hackathon, and meet the peeps behind the Nextflow pipelines that are open-source and community-driven.

This was the occasion to meet my colleague [Chris Wyatt]({{ site.baseurl }}/the-group/christopher-wyatt/), and former colleaguer [Iwo Pieniak](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ACoAACPyVB0B0w3Bip1UDOZkeTHqIn54NQ6dgNM). It was an incredibly valuable experience, both for the technical skills I gained and the connections I made with other professionals in the field.
