---
title: New paper highlighted in Genome Biology and Evolution
---
A new paper, first author [Emeline Favreau]({{ site.baseurl }}/the-group/emeline-favreau/), has been highlighted in the January edition of journal ***Genome Biology and Evolution***. Congratulations to Emeline, [Chris Wyatt]({{ site.baseurl }}/the-group/christopher-wyatt/) and Katie Geist on their momentous study on the molecular basis of sociality across bees and wasps.

Read the highlight [here](https://academic.oup.com/gbe/article/15/1/evac182/6976136), or find the paper itself [here](https://academic.oup.com/gbe/article/15/1/evac174/6926469).
