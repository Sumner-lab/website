Museums play a key role in bridging the communication gap between scientists and the public. Louisa’s project explored the potential of museums to positively reshape public perception of widely disliked organisms through the case study of the [*World of Wasps* exhibition](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/museums-collections/events/2025/jun/world-wasps) at the UCL Grant Museum of Zoology. She interviewed over 350 visitors to the museum in 2025-2026, to understand the power of exhibitions in communication and changing people’s hearts and minds about one of the world’s least loved insects, the wasps!
