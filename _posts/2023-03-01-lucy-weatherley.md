*MSci student Lucy Weatherley* *on her undergraduate literature review and current MSci research project with the Sumner Lab*

I originally joined the Sumner Lab for my bachelors literature review on the potential of signal detection theory in behavioural ecology, supervised by Professor [Seirian Summer]({{ site.baseurl }}/the-group/seirian-sumner/). Since completing this project I have now begun my master’s research, which focuses on the evolutionary patterns and processes of prey specialisms in crabronid hunting wasps.
