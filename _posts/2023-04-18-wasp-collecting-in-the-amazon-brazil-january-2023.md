In January of 2023, <a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/the-group/seirian-sumner/" data-type="page" data-id="12">Prof Seirian Sumner</a> and post-doc <a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/the-group/cintia-oi/" data-type="page" data-id="1298">Dr Cintia Oi</a> travelled to Brazil to carry out work as part of their project **Secrets to a Successful Hunt: Integrating Genomes, Chemistry and Behaviour in Neotropical Solitary Wasps**​ (read full details of this collaborative international NERC-funded project [here](https://gtr.ukri.org/projects?ref=NE%2FW004437%2F1)).

The main aims of this project are to:

- Generate genomes for solitary wasps
- Train Brazilian ecologists in bioinformatics ​
- Analyze the genomic basis of predator-prey sensory mechanisms and evolution

In São Paulo state, they connected with collaborators [Prof Fabio Nascimento](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Fabio-Nascimento-10)​ (Univ. Sao Paulo, USP-Ribeirao)​ and [Prof Marco Del Lama](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Marco-Del-Lama)​ (São Carlos Federal Univ, UFSCar-Sao Carlos​).

From Sao Paulo they travelled to Manaus, where they were greeted by Dr. Alexandre Somavilla, Prof Marcio Oliveira & co at [INPA](https://www.gov.br/inpa/pt-br), Manaus, who generously shared their wonderful wasp collections as well as their expertise.
