*UCL BSc student Tiffene Larose on her summer internship and final year project with the Sumner Lab*

I joined the Sumner lab in the summer of 2023 doing a research internship with <a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/the-group/owen-corbett/" data-type="page" data-id="971">Owen Corbett</a>, exploring physical traits of of paper wasps (*Polistes lanio*) and how they may link to life history and social hierarchies. Following this placement, I completed my own BSc research project under the supervision of <a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/the-group/seirian-sumner/" data-type="page" data-id="12">Prof. Seirian Sumner</a> and <a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/the-group/owen-corbett/" data-type="page" data-id="971">Owen Corbett</a>. My project utilised automated tracking of *Polistes* wasps on the nest, investigating the feasibility of [TRex](https://trex.run/docs/#) tracking software to extract tracking data from videos of wild nests taken in the field, and exploring the kinds of biologically-relevant data we can obtain from such automated tracking methods.

Working with the Sumner lab and CBER has been such a great experience and I have learnt so much from the researchers at the lab. 

*Tiffene has since graduated from her BSc in Biological Sciences with First Class Honours.*
