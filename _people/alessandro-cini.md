*Ale under a hover wasp cluster in Malaysia.*

**Project:** Insect social parasites: behavioural genomics models for understanding the basis of phenotypic evolution.

**Research interests**

I'm an ethologist interested in animal sociality and communication. I mainly focus on social insects (paper wasps, hornets and honeybees), but through collaborations I've also explored other systems, from invasive species like *Drosophila suzukii* to the biogeography of Mediterranean butterfly assemblages.

I combine behavioural, chemical and population genetic tools. During my PhD I studied the paper wasp social parasite-host system *Polistes sulcifer*–*Polistes dominula*, uncovering behavioural and chemical traits specific to the parasite or host. In the Sumner Lab, I explore the molecular basis of phenotypic evolution using *Polistes* social parasites and their hosts — parasites show striking phenotypic diversity compared to their hosts, having lost ancestral traits like the worker caste while gaining new ones adapted to exploiting host resources.
